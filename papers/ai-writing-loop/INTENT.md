## 1) Intent (what this paper does)
- Argue for replacing human-heavy writing cycles with an automated pipeline that produces executive-grade strategy artifacts.
- Show why the loop scales: parallelism, repeatability, auditability, and measurable quality.
- Provide a concrete implementation pattern: intent/spec → deterministic checks → low-cost semantic feedback → full-review gate → iterative patch loop → publish.

## 2) Definition of done (“polished”)

- Recommendations are specific and testable (owner, timeline, metric)
- No placeholders/TODOs
- Clear through-line from problem → understand → decision → action
- Includes references and at least one illustrative example workflow

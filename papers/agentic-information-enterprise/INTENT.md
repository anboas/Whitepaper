> Single source of truth for what this paper is supposed to be.

## 0) Identity
- **Title:** The Agentic Information Enterprise: A DoW CIO Operating Concept for Governed Workforce Creation
- **Subtitle:** A DoW CIO operating concept for assigning, supervising, assuring, measuring, and scaling governed non-person agent capacity across the DoD Information Enterprise
- **Author name:** Adam Boas
- **Author role:** Agentic Warfare Architect, Department of War
- **Date:** August 2026
- **Audience:** DoW CIO, CDAO, enterprise architecture, ICAM, cyber authorization, records, portfolio, policy, and digital workforce leaders
- **Distribution:** Public

## 1) Intent (what this paper does)
- Define the institutional operating model for treating governed non-person agents as assignable workforce capacity.
- Bridge ACP-RA and CAF-RA into a CIO-level employment, supervision, acceptance, evidence, and capacity model.
- Establish DoW CIO as the correct enterprise proving ground without claiming sole ownership of all Department agent work.
- Preserve human authority for reserved decisions while allowing bounded Work Units, evidence production, routing, and review support.

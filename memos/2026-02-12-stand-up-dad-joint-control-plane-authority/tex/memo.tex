\textbf{DECISION.}

Approve immediate standup of a \textbf{Delegated Autonomy Directorate
(DAD)} as a joint authority chartered at Secretary level and overseen by
the Deputy Secretary, and direct a 90-day rollout of an Agent Control
Plane baseline for high-consequence agentic operations {[}1{]}.

\textbf{BLUF.}

This is not a software cycle. It is a warfighting control shift.

Properly designed, DAD is not a brake on innovation or speed, but a
force accelerator that increases capability and operational tempo by
making delegated autonomy trusted, repeatable, and safe to scale.

The Department is moving from force multiplication (human-hours
amplified) to force creation (agent-hours delegated) {[}2{]}. Force
creation removes human reaction time as the bottleneck; operational
scale decouples from personnel and scales through compute, access, and
permissions. In contested conditions, decision tempo and action density
can exceed human relevance windows. The side that can delegate authority
safely at machine speed will set operational tempo.

\textbf{ORGANIZING CONSTRUCT.}

DAD should be dual-homed and backed by acquisition leverage:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Technical enforcement:} CIO-IE (identity, ABAC, gateways,
  policy-as-code, cATO alignment)
\item
  \textbf{Operational doctrine:} Joint Staff / C2 authority (consequence
  tiers, escalation triggers, degraded-mode doctrine)
\item
  \textbf{Acquisition leverage:} A\&S / procurement hooks (no funding
  without conformance)
\end{itemize}

\textbf{REQUIRED AUTHORITIES.}

The Directorate must be empowered to:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Mandate Trust Scope Manifests
\item
  Block deployment for non-conformance
\item
  Revoke scopes across enclaves
\item
  Impose degraded-mode posture
\item
  Require eval-pack conformance before scale
\item
  Run red-team drills on autonomy
\item
  Suspend tool registries or inter-agent channels
\end{itemize}

\textbf{90-DAY EXECUTION ORDER.}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{0-30 days:} Sign joint charter; designate authorities; publish
  minimum ACP baseline profile.
\item
  \textbf{31-60 days:} Field enforcement plumbing and doctrine gates;
  run two controlled fieldings.
\item
  \textbf{61-90 days:} Enforce no-conformance/no-scale rule; execute
  containment and red-team drills; publish command scorecard.
\end{itemize}

This is not an ``AI program.'' It is the institutionalization of
machine-executed authority. That authority must live where control
doctrine and enforcement infrastructure intersect, above any single
functional lane. The control plane is the new nervous system; it cannot
be owned by a single organ, and \textbf{any domain where governed force
creation is not achieved first is a domain where enduring advantage is
at risk}.
